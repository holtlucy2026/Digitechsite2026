At Coastal Veterinary Services, we believe veterinary care should be calm, personal and centred on your pet’s comfort and wellbeing. Our Coolum Beach consult rooms have been thoughtfully designed to provide a welcoming and relaxed environment for pets and their families. As a quieter alternative to a traditional veterinary clinic, consultations are focused on creating a positive experience while allowing time to understand your pet’s individual needs. Whether you’re visiting for a routine health check, vaccination, medical concern or ongoing health management, you’ll receive personalised care from Dr Sandra every step of the way.

Continuity of Care

One of the benefits of Coastal Veterinary Services is knowing who will be caring for your pet. All consultations are personally provided by Dr Sandra, allowing genuine relationships to develop with pets and their families over time. This continuity of care means a veterinarian who understands your pet’s history, health needs and individual personality. By seeing the same veterinarian throughout your pet’s life, we can build trust, provide consistency and develop treatment plans tailored to your pet’s individual needs.

Mobile Veterinary Services

Convenient veterinary care in the comfort of your home.

We bring personalised veterinary care to your doorstep, offering home visit services from Noosa to Marcoola. For some pets, being seen at home can make all the difference. Familiar surroundings often help reduce stress and allow consultations to take place in an environment where pets feel comfortable and secure. Personally provided by Dr Sandra, our home visit service offers the same personalised care and attention as our Coolum Beach consult rooms, with the added convenience of veterinary care at home.

Which Pets Benefit From Home Visits?

Home visits can be particularly beneficial for: Senior pets Pets with mobility challenges Anxious or nervous pets Multi-pet households Pets that become stressed travelling in the car Families seeking a more convenient consultation option Being able to assess your pet in their home environment can also provide valuable insight into their daily routine, behaviour and overall wellbeing.
