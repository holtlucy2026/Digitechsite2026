At Coastal Veterinary Services, we believe veterinary care should be calm, personal and centred on your pet’s comfort and wellbeing. Whether you visit our Coolum Beach consult rooms, choose the convenience of a home visit, or need compassionate end-of-life support, you’ll receive thoughtful care tailored to your pet’s individual needs.

Coolum Beach Consult Rooms. Our private consult rooms have been thoughtfully designed to create a relaxed, welcoming environment where pets and their families can feel at ease. Ideal for routine health care, medical consultations and pets who benefit from a quieter experience.

Mobile Vet Service. Veterinary care brought to your doorstep. For pets who are anxious, elderly, less mobile, or simply happier at home, we provide house-call veterinary services from Noosa to Marcoola. Enjoy personalised care without the stress of travel, waiting rooms or unfamiliar surroundings.

From puppy and kitten health checks to senior pet care and compassionate end-of-life support, Coastal Veterinary Services is here to help your pet live comfortably and be cared for with kindness every step of the way. Because great veterinary care isn’t just about medicine — it’s about trust, compassion and feeling supported when it matters most.
